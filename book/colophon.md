# Colophon

This is a book of the books on Kristian Bjørnard’s office shelves at MICA, filed A to Z by title as they stand there. Each spine is drawn at {scale} of its size, from library catalogues (Open Library and the Library of Congress), from its page count, or, failing those, a guess.

It is also a type specimen. Each spine is set in one of thirteen typefaces, all free and open source under the SIL Open Font License: anyone can use, study, change and share them. A series shares a face, and so, mostly, does a publisher. Here is each one, set in itself, and where to get it:
