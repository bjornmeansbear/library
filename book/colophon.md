# Colophon

This is a book of the books in Kristian Bjørnard’s library catalog, in alphabetical order. Each spine is drawn at {scale} of its size, from measurements taken off library catalogs (Open Library and the Library of Congress), estimated from its page count, or, failing those, a guess.

It is also a miniature type specimen. Each spine is set in one of thirteen free/libre and open source typefaces, most under the SIL Open Font License: anyone can use, study, change and share them. Here is each one, set in itself, and where to get it:

{faces}

All the info was drawn from library.wjerk.shop on {date}; my whole catalog as a usable data source is open at library.wjerk.shop/library.json. The line across the top of the shelf is from Terry Pratchett’s *Soul Music* (1994). The engraving of a book is in the public domain. Typeset with Bjook (HTML and CSS, pdf-ed by WeasyPrint), bjornmeansbook.com. Any new text by Kristian Bjørnard is CC BY-SA 4.0. The books themselves, their titles and everything else about them, belong to their authors and publishers. A Wjerk project.
